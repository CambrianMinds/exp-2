# Indiana Rules of Trial Procedure: Document Formatting Guide for AI Agents

**Objective:** When generating legal pleadings, motions, memoranda, and briefs for Indiana courts, you must strictly apply the following structural and formatting constraints governed by Indiana Trial Rule 10.

## 1. Page Layout

* **Size:** Produce documents sized at standard 8.5 x 11 inches [cite: 1.2.1].
* **Margins:** Maintain at least a one-inch margin on all four sides of the text (top, bottom, left, and right) [cite: 1.2.1].

## 2. Typography Requirements

* **Font Size:** Both body text and footnotes must be 12-point or larger [cite: 1.2.1].
* **Color:** All output text must be strictly black [cite: 1.2.1].
* **Acceptable Typefaces:** You must limit font selection to the officially permitted list [cite: 1.2.1]. Safe defaults include Times New Roman, Arial, Garamond, Century Schoolbook, or Georgia [cite: 1.2.1]. (Other permitted fonts: Baskerville, Book Antiqua, Bookman, Bookman Old Style, Century, Calisto MT, CG Times, New Baskerville, New Century Schoolbook, and Palatino) [cite: 1.2.1].

## 3. Spacing Rules

* **Default Spacing:** All standard body text must be double-spaced [cite: 1.2.1].
* **Exceptions:** You must use single-spacing for footnotes, blocked-and-indented text (such as long block quotes), tables, charts, and similar structured material [cite: 1.2.1].

## 4. Pagination

* **Numbering:** Pages must be numbered consecutively [cite: 1.2.1].
* **Placement:** Page numbers must be located at the bottom of the page [cite: 1.2.1].
* **Format:** Numbering must begin with numeral one (1) on the first page [cite: 1.2.1].

## 5. Caption Structure

Every pleading must feature a caption setting forth the following elements:

1. The exact name of the court [cite: 1.2.1].
2. The title of the action (the names of the parties) [cite: 1.2.1].
3. The file number / cause number [cite: 1.2.1].
4. A Rule 7(A) designation (identifying the specific nature of the pleading, e.g., "Motion to Dismiss") [cite: 1.2.1].
